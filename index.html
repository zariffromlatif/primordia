Primordia

An island where creatures with tiny neural brains graze, hunt, learn, court mates, call to each other and split into new species, evolving by Darwin’s natural and sexual selection while rain carves rivers through the land. Nothing is scripted: every behavior you see was inherited or learned.

Year 1, spring

Circles graze and triangles hunt. Grazers must learn which plants, gold or blue, are poisonous this season. Fan-shaped tails are ornaments that attract mates. Yellow and blue halos are the two calls, and a purple core means plague. Pale clouds are storms; the rain they drop runs off into rivers and lakes.
