Primordia

An island where creatures with tiny neural brains graze, hunt, pair up, call to each other and split into new species, while rain carves rivers through the land. Nothing is scripted: every behavior you see was inherited.

Year 1, spring

Circles graze, triangles hunt anything smaller than themselves, a tail is an ornament for winning mates, a yellow halo means a creature is calling, and a purple core means it’s sick. Pale clouds are storms; the rain they drop runs off into rivers and lakes, where thirsty creatures come to drink.
